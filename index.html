How the Pomodoro Technique Works

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo that uses a timer to break work into intervals, traditionally 25 minutes in length, separated by short breaks.

01

Set Your Timer

Choose a task and set the timer for 25 minutes (a standard Pomodoro)

02

Focus Deeply

Work on your task with complete focus until the timer rings

03

Take a Break

Take a short 5-minute break to rest your mind

04

Repeat & Track

Repeat the cycle and track your productivity over time
